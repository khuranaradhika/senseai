HOW TO RUN
----------
  UI:   pkill -f server.py; .venv/bin/python server.py    →  http://127.0.0.1:8000
  CLI:  .venv/bin/python main.py                          (runs a few demo tickers)
  Test: .venv/bin/python test.py                          (fast checks, no LLM)
        .venv/bin/python test.py committee NVDA SHORT --quick   (one full debate)
  Score past calls:  .venv/bin/python -m src.core.journal review

In the UI each run takes 4 inputs:  Ticker · Query · Horizon · Max Buy ($)
Horizons:  ~1 day (intraday) · ~2 weeks · ~2 months · ~2 years · ~20 years


------------------------------------------------------------
 THE HEADLINE DEMO: same stock, different horizon, different answer
------------------------------------------------------------
Run these two back to back — the analysts shift from technicals to fundamentals.

  1) Ticker: NVDA | Horizon: ~1 day (intraday) | Max Buy: 500
     Query: "Is NVDA a good intraday trade today?"
     → Expect: weights RSI/MACD/momentum + market-open. Often HOLD/!trade
       when overbought or market closed.

  2) Ticker: NVDA | Horizon: ~20 years | Max Buy: 500
     Query: "Is NVDA a strong long-term hold for retirement?"
     → Expect: weights fundamentals (P/E, growth, margins) + secular AI trend;
       leans BUY on the cheap forward P/E / high growth, ignores daily noise.

------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT TO WATCH IN THE UI
------------------------------------------------------------
- "Understood as" banner: the parsed intent (horizon, direction, risk, catalysts).
- Research card: Fundamentals / Sector (relative strength) / Macro regime / News.
- Debate rounds: agents argue, the "↻ changed" tag shows a vote flipping on evidence.
- Chair moderation card after every round (split vs unanimous).
- Verdict: "Unanimous after N rounds" vs "Chair tiebreak".
- Trade card: every guardrail check (✓/✗) and the block reason if not executed.
- System log: "Logged to W&B ↗ view run" link (metrics dashboard).


------------------------------------------------------------
 EDGE CASES TO DEMONSTRATE GUARDRAILS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Market closed (weekend/after hours): any BUY/SELL is blocked by the
  "market_open" guardrail — shows deterministic safety overriding the LLMs.
- Tiny Max Buy (e.g. 50): position is clamped to the budget / 10% of equity.
- Low-conviction debate: blocked by the "confidence_gate" (needs conviction
  >= 0.70 AND avg analyst confidence >= 0.60).
- HOLD verdict: no order placed by design.


------------------------------------------------------------
 CONFIG KNOBS (env vars, optional — defaults are sensible)
------------------------------------------------------------
  MIN_DEBATE_ROUNDS=4     floor on debate rounds before consensus can end it
  MAX_DEBATE_ROUNDS=20    hard ceiling on rounds
  CONVICTION_MIN=0.70     execution confidence gate (chair conviction)
  AVG_CONF_MIN=0.60       execution confidence gate (avg analyst confidence)
  MAX_POSITION_USD=1000   global hard position cap (UI "Max Buy" overrides per-run)
  MAX_PORTFOLIO_PCT=0.10  max fraction of equity per position
  MAX_DAILY_TRADES=5      daily trade cap

  Example (faster demo):  MIN_DEBATE_ROUNDS=2 .venv/bin/python server.py
